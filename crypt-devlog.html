Development notes and progress updates for Crypt.

About the Project

Crypt is a capstone project for the Mohawk College Game Design/Development course. It is a roguelite dungeon crawler with looter shooter systems.

Development Log

November 1, 2026 — Crypt so far.

Crypt has been a challenge to create, requiring me to research and implement 3D procedural dungeon generation and inventory systems. Both of these systems are massively important to the gameplay experience of Crypt: you can't loot without an inventory system, and you can't fight through non existant dungeons!

Inventory Systems

Dungeon Generation

I created a dungeon generation system that uses a three dimensonal grid of Vector3Ints to track cell occupation. Cells each create their own new script, which tracks the information given to it by the generator. For example, a cell knows which rooms it connects to and where it was entered from. This allows the later spawned prefabs to properly enable/disable walls, doors and other assets within the room. This system allowed for massive mazelike dungeons, although they were not particularly interesting, especially with the rough visual style. To expand from this point I began to make modules, which are rooms that can occupy any number of cells, rather then just one. These modules reserve their footprint, and the dungeon keeps track of where their entrances are for future use. Since the entrances are placed within the prefab, I could theoratically make massive arenas with a lot of exits and the dungeon would be able to connect to any of them to continue it's generation. This is massive boon to the interest of our dungeon generation! Now we can have handcrafted challenges, visually distinct shapes and actual level design in the dungeon.

Next steps

I am currently working on reworking the visuals of the dungeon to reach a higher fidelity and flesh out the liminal feeling that we want for our dungeon. I will post another devlog diving into this work soon!
